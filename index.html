Chains run downhill

A flipped tile topples every neighbour holding a lower number, and those topple theirs. Equal numbers block. Nines are fortresses, zeros are scalpels.

Certified fair

Every level ships with a proof.

Before a puzzle reaches you, a solver searches it exhaustively. It has to be solvable, its par has to be exact, and its optional master goal has to be reachable. The proof is stored next to the level and re-checked on every build.

No board is probably fine. That is why there are no hint packs to sell you: unlimited undo and a solver-powered hint are simply built in.

The ritual

One board. The whole world. Every day.

Today is Daily Flip #251, September 8. The same five-by-five for everyone, everywhere, built on your device from the date alone. There is no server behind it and no account to make.

Keep a streak and put the board itself on your Home Screen. The large widget shows today's grid and the four numbers in your hand, so you can plan the cascade before you open the app, and raises your cleared board as a trophy once you solve it. On iOS 27 it goes extra-large and fills the screen. Miss a day and you get one free repair a month, because life happens.

Seasons

Autumn: every leaf falls
in its own time.

A season is a dated run of boards that opens one a day and then stays yours forever. Autumn is the first: fifteen boards from October 1, built around one new tile, with warmer paper, leaves drifting past the board and a cascade tuned to a minor key.

The leaf loses a number every time a placement resolves. Flip it while it still stands, or let it reach zero, fall, and wall that square off for good. Every board is certified the same way the campaign is: par exact, witness stored, and the leaf proven to matter.

› › ›

One leaf, one placement at a time, and the move after zero drops it. A fallen leaf is a stone: it never flips again, and nothing cascades through it.

One board a day, October 1 to October 15.

Every board is free for everyone while the season runs, unlocked or not. When it closes the pack joins the full game, and anything you opened stays yours either way.

The flex

One move. Flip the most.

One-Shot hands you a dense board and a single tile. Bombs, wormholes and rollovers turn the obvious placement into a trap. Find the one that beats the board's certified best, then share the whole cascade as a looping GIF from the win screen.

Every board is a link. Send one to a friend and they can play the exact same puzzle, no install required.
