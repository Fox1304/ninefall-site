The game

Every tile holds a number. Play one of yours onto it — the values add, the tile flips, and every lower-numbered neighbor topples after it, chaining down the board like dominoes. Clear it all with a handful of numbers.

One rule hides the depth: past 9, numbers roll over. 6 + 6 = 2. Greed has a price, nines are fortresses, zeros are scalpels.

Six worlds, 72 machine-certified levels, five hidden secrets, and the Daily Flip — one board, the whole world, every day.

Support

Found a bug, hit a wall, or want to suggest something? Open an issue and it will be read — Ninefall is made by one person, so replies are human and reasonably quick.

Undo is unlimited and every level is provably solvable in its par — if one seems impossible, the hint button knows the way.

Privacy

Ninefall collects no data. None.

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of any kind, and no account to create. Your progress lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud key-value storage that only you and your devices can read. Nothing is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected” because there is genuinely nothing to collect.
