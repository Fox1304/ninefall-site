One rule hides the depth

Numbers add when tiles meet. Past nine, they roll over. 6 + 6 = 2.

Play a tile, the values add, the tile flips, and every lower-numbered neighbor topples after it, chaining down the board like dominoes. Greed has a price, nines are fortresses, zeros are scalpels.

The journey

Six worlds. Every level certified fair.

72 handcrafted puzzles taught one idea at a time: stones, seals, gems, ice, bombs, snares, wormholes. Our solver proves every level is solvable in its par before it ships, and stores the proof. Five secret levels hide off the map for gem hunters.

The ritual

One board, the whole world, every day.

The Daily Flip is the same board for everyone, everywhere, once a day. Keep a streak, compare par with friends, and put tomorrow's board on your Home Screen with the widget. Miss a day? One free streak repair every month, because life happens.

The feel

Made like a toy.

Chunky ceramic tiles that flip with real weight. Haptics choreographed to every topple, marimba notes that climb as your chain grows, and a slow-motion finale when the whole board goes. 100 percent native, tiny download, zero third-party code.

The flex

One move. Flip the most.

One-Shot mode hands you a dense board and a single tile. Bombs, wormholes, and stones turn the obvious play into a trap; find the placement that beats the certified best, then share the whole cascade as a looping GIF straight from the win screen.

Privacy

Ninefall collects no data. None.

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of any kind, and no account to create. Your progress lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud storage that only you can read. The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected” because there is genuinely nothing to collect.
